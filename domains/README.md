The two resolved MGL candidates have no domain-validation records in the historical analysis because domain reconfirmation was configured for the three pre-resolution MGL search bins, not the post-resolution `mgl` family. The deposited ledger makes that absence explicit and retains both candidates under the gate's no-configured-rule default.
